➕ About QuestLab

QuestLab is a gamified learning platform developed for Passenger Service students at MBO College Airport.

The platform combines retrieval practice, microlearning, challenge-based learning and immediate feedback to help students retain knowledge in a fun and engaging way.

QuestLab started in February 2026 as a simple IATA code game and has grown into a complete aviation learning platform featuring multiple games, XP, ranks and badges.
