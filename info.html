QuestLab Information

➕ About QuestLab

QuestLab is a gamified learning platform developed for Passenger Service students at MBO College Airport.

The platform combines retrieval practice, microlearning, challenge-based learning and immediate feedback to help students retain knowledge in a fun and engaging way.

QuestLab started in February 2026 as a simple IATA code game and has grown into a complete aviation learning platform featuring multiple games, XP, ranks and badges.

➕ Learning through Play

QuestLab is built on the principle that students learn best when they are actively engaged. Research on gamification, retrieval practice and microlearning shows that short, repeated learning activities improve long-term retention and motivation. QuestLab uses:
  • Retrieval practice
  • Immediate feedback
  • Timed challenges
  • XP and ranks
  • Self-paced learning
  • Challenge-based learning
The goal is not to replace teachers, but to support classroom instruction and encourage students to practice outside lessons.

➕ Meet the Games

QuestLab currently features a growing collection of aviation-themed learning games, each designed to support specific knowledge and skills required in Passenger Services. 🌍 Topo Quest Students explore the world by learning countries, capitals and major cities. Topography remains an essential skill in aviation, helping future Passenger Service Agents understand destinations, routes and global geography. 🧩 IATA Code QuestMatch Players match airport codes with their corresponding cities. The game uses retrieval practice and timed challenges to help students master hundreds of airport codes used throughout the aviation industry. ✈️ AIRIMP QuestMatch Students learn Special Service Requests (SSR), meal codes, ticket statuses, baggage codes and operational terminology based on the AIRIMP manual used by airlines worldwide. 🛬 Airline QuestMatch Players learn airline names, IATA and ICAO carrier codes and their countries of origin. Students at MBO College Airport practice a curated set of airlines used in their curriculum, while QuestLab also supports a complete international airline database. 📨 Airline Message Quest Students build and interpret aviation messages, developing familiarity with the structured communication used throughout the airline industry. 🧭 IATA Code Charades A mobile-only team game where students physically act out airport codes, encouraging movement, collaboration and active recall. • AIRIMP Charades Students work together to guess aviation terms, transforming traditional classroom revision into an engaging and memorable activity. Together, these games combine microlearning, retrieval practice, immediate feedback and gamification to help students learn faster, retain knowledge longer and enjoy the learning process.

➕ Ranks & XP

Every completed mission awards Experience Points (XP). As players earn XP, they progress through the QuestLab ranks:
  • Junior Flight Attendant
  • Flight Attendant
  • Senior Flight Attendant
  • Assistant Purser
  • Purser
  • Senior Purser
Ranks are designed to reflect career progression within the aviation industry. QuestLab encourages students to practice regularly, celebrate progress and develop confidence in their aviation knowledge.
